Privacy Policy for Chalk That NFL

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Chalk That NFL ("the App," "we," "us") is developed and maintained by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

To provide account-based features such as login and personalized access, the App collects:

  • Email Address — used to create and authenticate your account.
  • User ID / Account Identifier — an internal identifier used to associate your account with your data within the App.

We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, location, health or fitness data, financial or payment information, contacts, photos, or browsing/search history from outside the App.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for App Functionality, including to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep you signed in
  • Associate your saved data and preferences with your account
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the App and its backend services

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, product personalization, or marketing communications. We do not use any third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in this App.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your account data is stored on our own backend servers and is not shared with advertising networks, data brokers, or other outside parties.

Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, please contact us using the information below.

No Real-Money Transactions

Chalk That NFL is a sports research and analytics tool. It does not process wagers, bets, or real-money transactions of any kind.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
